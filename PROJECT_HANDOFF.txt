WALLSTREET v4.1 — latest full build

Project: mobile-first pixel-art PvP game about financial firms and an internal player-driven market, styled after Wall Street offices of the 1980s–1990s.

Architecture:
- index.html — entry point
- app.js — orchestrator only; should remain the "head" of the app, not a dumping ground
- styles/ — split CSS modules
- js/core/ — state/helpers
- js/components/ — HUD/nav/overlays/icons
- js/data/ — static office object definitions
- js/views/ — office, market, finance, company, city, inventory
- js/systems/ — world/weather/CRT/market engine/chart/storage
- assets/ — office, furniture, characters, props, city layers

Current visual reference: the supplied premium vertical pixel-art Wall Street office image. The office must feel like a living interactive world, not a flat menu.

Latest v4.1 layout pass:
- rebalanced central Trading Desk scale and placement
- repositioned Analyst / Risk / Finance / Lounge / TV / Exit zones
- moved characters closer to their workstations
- reduced CRT canvas overlays so they sit inside monitor glass
- reduced hotspot size
- moved Next Market lower to avoid HUD overlap

Core game rules to preserve:
- office is the primary interactive interface
- player-driven PvP markets; price movement comes from participant order flow versus market depth
- no RNG-generated price path in the actual PvP mode
- SOL = trading capital; Credits = progression/cosmetics/tools, not direct profit multipliers
- mobile-first vertical design
- do not turn the market screen into Binance/TradingView
